# Privacy Policy for Marvel Industries (SleepEye)

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

Marvel Industries ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our SleepEye mobile application collects, uses, and discloses information. 

**IMPORTANT NOTICE: INTERNAL BUSINESS USE ONLY**
The SleepEye application is a proprietary, internal business tool developed exclusively for authorized employees, contractors, and registered business partners of Marvel Industries. It is **not intended for use by the general public**. Access to the app is strictly limited to authorized personnel with pre-approved credentials.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
Because this is an internal business application, user accounts are created and managed by Marvel Industries administrators. Information associated with your account may include:
- **Employee/Partner Information:** Name, corporate email address, phone number, and internal employee/partner ID.
- **Business Operations Data:** Data entered into the app during the course of business tasks related to our SleepEye bed sheet products.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
When authorized personnel use the app, we automatically collect certain telemetry and usage data for IT security and performance monitoring:
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- **Usage Data:** App interactions, crash logs, and timestamps of business activities.

### C. Device Permissions
To facilitate internal business operations, the app requests the following Android permissions:
- **Camera:** Required to scan product barcodes, QR codes, or capture images of inventory/products for business reporting.
- **Storage / Gallery (Read/Write External Storage & Media):** Required to upload business-related documents or photos from your device, or to save app-generated reports locally.
- **Internet / Network State:** Required to communicate securely with our internal enterprise servers and databases.

*Note: These permissions are used strictly for performing authorized business tasks. We do not access your camera or storage for any personal or non-business purposes.*

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for internal corporate purposes:
- To facilitate daily business operations, inventory management, and task tracking for SleepEye products.
- To verify the identity of authorized personnel accessing corporate systems.
- To monitor app performance, troubleshoot IT issues, and maintain corporate data security.
- To maintain audit logs of business activities.

## 3. Data Safety and Security

We employ strict enterprise-grade security measures to protect corporate data:
- **Encryption:** All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
- **Access Control:** Data is stored on secure corporate servers accessible only to authorized IT and administrative personnel.
- **Data Deletion:** Upon termination of employment or business partnership, your access to the app will be revoked and associated personal data will be deleted or anonymized in accordance with Marvel Industries' internal corporate data retention policies. You may also contact your IT administrator to request data review or deletion.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

Data collected through this app is treated as confidential corporate information. We **do not sell** any data. We only share data in the following cases:
- **Service Providers:** With trusted third-party enterprise vendors (e.g., secure cloud hosting) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect the legal rights of Marvel Industries.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Because this is a corporate business tool, it is strictly intended for adult employees and authorized partners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this internal Privacy Policy as our business operations evolve. We will notify authorized users of significant changes via internal corporate communication channels (e.g., corporate email or intranet announcements).
