# Privacy Policy for Marvel Industries (SleepEye)

**Effective Date:** September 11, 2026

Marvel Industries ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our SleepEye mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **User Account Data:** Name, email address, phone number, and business details needed to manage orders and user accounts.
- **Order & Business Data:** Product orders, dimensions, invoices, and payment/transaction details.

### B. Device Permissions & Media Access
To facilitate order creation and business tasks, the app requests:
- **Camera:** Required when you choose to take a photo of products, invoices, or barcodes directly using the camera.
- **Photo Picker:** When uploading product photos or order attachments from your gallery, the app uses Android's secure system Photo Picker. The app does NOT request or access broad media storage (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`/`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`) or personal files.
- **Internet / Network State:** Required to communicate securely with our cloud servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To manage SleepEye product orders, inventory, and dealer accounts.
- To provide customer support and process invoices.
- To ensure security and prevent fraudulent transactions.

## 3. Data Safety & Security
- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
- **No Data Selling:** We do not sell user data to third parties or advertising networks.
- **Data Deletion:** You can request the deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us.
